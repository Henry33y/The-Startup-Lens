**🔭 PLATFORM OVERVIEW (Product Vision)**

**Core Idea:**

“The Bloomberg \+ YouTube \+ LinkedIn for early-stage African builders.”

But sharper:

* Not polished success → **raw progress**  
* Not noise → **signal**  
* Not audience → **community with identity**

**🔑 Product Layers (Think in Systems)**

1. **Media Layer (Content Engine)**  
   * Articles, founder stories, podcasts, short videos  
   * Editorial \+ user-generated  
2. **Builder Layer (Profiles \+ Identity)**  
   * Every founder has a living profile  
   * Tracks their journey over time  
3. **Discovery Layer (Algorithm \+ Curation)**  
   * Surfaces underrated builders early  
4. **Community Layer (Interaction \+ Belonging)**  
   * Comments, reactions, discussions, follow system  
5. **Data Layer (Insights \+ Trends)**  
   * “Who’s building what in Africa right now?”

👉 This is what makes you different:  
 You’re not publishing content. You’re **mapping an ecosystem in real time.**

**🏗️ TECH ARCHITECTURE (Scalable, realistic)**

You’re on MERN—good. But upgrade it.

**🔧 Core Stack**

* **Frontend:** React (Next.js recommended)  
* **Backend:** Node.js \+ Express  
* **Database:** MongoDB (with proper indexing)  
* **Auth:** Firebase Auth or Auth0

---

**⚡ Enhancements**

**1\. Search & Discovery**

* Use **Algolia** or **Elasticsearch**  
   → Fast, intelligent search

---

**2\. Real-Time Features**

* Socket.io  
   → Notifications, live updates

---

**3\. Media Handling**

* Cloudinary (videos \+ images)

---

**4\. AI Layer**

* APIs (OpenAI / Groq / HF models)  
* Microservice for AI tasks

---

**5\. Feed Algorithm**

* Custom ranking service  
* Cached with Redis
